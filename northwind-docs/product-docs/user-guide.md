# NorthwindFlow User Guide (version 3.2)

> Fictional demo data.

## The basics

- **Workspace:** your company's space in NorthwindFlow. Admins invite people.
- **Project:** a goal with tasks, for example "Website launch."
- **Board:** shows a project's tasks in columns (To do, Doing, Done). Drag cards to update them.
- **Task:** has an owner, a due date, comments, and files.

## Signing in (new in 3.2)

- Go to app.northwind.example or open the app.
- Choose **Sign in with email**, or **Sign in with company SSO** if your company uses single sign-on (Team plan).
- After updating to 3.2, everyone is signed out once and must sign in again.

## Notifications

You get a notification when someone assigns you a task, mentions you (@name), or changes a due date. Change this under Settings → Notifications.

## Syncing

Your work syncs across all your devices within seconds. NorthwindFlow needs an internet connection; without one, it shows "You're offline" and changes can't be saved.

## Admin settings (Team plan)

- Invite or remove people: Settings → Members.
- Turn on company SSO: Settings → Security → Single sign-on. Your SSO provider must list NorthwindFlow's sign-in address: https://login.northwind.example/sso/callback

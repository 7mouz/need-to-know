# Northwind Values

> Fictional demo data.

Four values guide how we work with each other, with our customers, and with our partners. Each one says what we believe and what it means for anyone who works with us.

## 1. Customer focus

**What we believe:** Our customers' time is the scarcest thing they have. Every feature, fix, and reply should save them some.

**Why it makes us a good partner:** We reply to every support ticket, tell customers about known problems and their fixes as soon as we have them, and let customer requests shape what we build next.

## 2. Trust

**What we believe:** People share information with us because they trust us to protect it. Inside the company we share information on a need-to-know basis: each team, and each AI assistant, sees what its job needs.

**Why it makes us a good partner:** Customer and partner data reaches only the people and tools that need it. We can show who can see what, and we test it regularly.

## 3. Teamwork

**What we believe:** Hard problems get solved across teams. We ask early, share credit, and hand work off cleanly.

**Why it makes us a good partner:** You deal with one coordinated company. When a question belongs to another team, we route it to the right people instead of guessing.

## 4. Innovation

**What we believe:** Small, frequent improvements beat big, rare launches. We try new tools, measure the results, and keep what works.

**Why it makes us a good partner:** We improve NorthwindFlow every few months, and we adopt new technology carefully: we test it before it touches customer data.

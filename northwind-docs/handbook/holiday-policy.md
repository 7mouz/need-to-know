# Time Off and Holidays

> Fictional demo data.

| Type | Employees |
|---|---|
| Vacation | 20 days per year (prorated in your first year) |
| Sick days | 8 days per year |
| Company holidays | 11 days per year (list below) |
| Carry-over | Up to 5 unused vacation days into next year |

## How to request time off

1. Open PeoplePort (the HR portal) → Time off → New request.
2. Ask at least 2 weeks ahead for vacations longer than 3 days.
3. Your manager approves it in PeoplePort.

## Company holidays (2026)

New Year's Day · Martin Luther King Jr. Day · Presidents' Day · Memorial Day · Juneteenth · Independence Day · Labor Day · Thanksgiving and the day after · Christmas Eve · Christmas Day

## Contractors

Contractors follow the time-off terms in their own contract. Tell your Northwind contact about planned absences at least 1 week ahead.

# Office Handbook

> Fictional demo data.

For everyone who works at Northwind: employees and contractors.

## Hours

- The office is open 7 AM to 7 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Core hours are 10 AM to 4 PM. Outside core hours, work when it suits your team.

## Getting in

- Your badge opens the front door and the 3rd-floor office. Lost your badge? Tell the front desk the same day.
- Visitors sign in at the front desk and wear a visitor sticker.

## Desks and rooms

- Desks are shared, first come, first served. Clear your desk at the end of the day.
- Book meeting rooms on the "Rooms" board in NorthwindFlow.

## Wi-Fi

- Staff: "Northwind-Staff". Sign in with your company account.
- Guests: "Northwind-Guest". The front desk gives guests the current access card; it changes every Monday.

## Working from home

- You can work from home up to 3 days a week. Agree on the days with your team.
- To reach internal tools from home, connect to the company VPN first. The setup steps are in the **IT runbook**.

## Kitchen

- Coffee, tea, and snacks are free. Please clean up after yourself.
- The kitchen is being renovated October 12–23. Use the 2nd-floor break room.

## Who to ask

- Building, badges, rooms: front desk (frontdesk@northwind.example)
- Laptops, VPN, accounts: IT helpdesk (see the IT runbook)
- Time off and pay questions: People team (people@northwind.example)

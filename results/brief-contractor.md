# First-week brief: contractor

## Who we are
Northwind Inc. makes NorthwindFlow, a project-management app for small and mid-size teams. Founded in 2019, we have about 120 employees and over 1,000 customer teams.

## Your first week
1. **Laptop:** Pick up your new laptop on your first day at the IT desk on the 3rd floor.
2. **VPN:** Install NorthwindConnect via HelpHub to connect to internal tools from home.
3. **Tool Access:** Request tools (like NorthwindFlow or PeoplePort) through HelpHub; your manager approves requests within one business day.
4. **Time Off:** Contractors follow their contract terms; notify your Northwind contact at least one week ahead of planned absences.

## What's happening now
1. **NorthwindFlow 3.2:** Shipped on September 22 with a new sign-in page and faster task boards.
2. **VPN Update:** Reminder to update NorthwindConnect to version 5.1+ by October 15, 2026.
3. **Kitchen Renovation:** The main kitchen is renovating October 12–23; use the 2nd-floor break room.
4. **Offline Mode:** Supporting offline work is a major product focus for 2027 under the theme "work anywhere."

## Who to ask for what
- Laptops, VPN, accounts: IT helpdesk
- Badges and office access: Front desk
- Pay and time off: People team (HR)
- Product plans and release notes: Product team
- Customer tickets and support playbook: Support team

# First-week brief: hr

> Note: these results were made when the demo company was still called Northwind. It is now PixelHeaven (products PixelFlow and PixelConnect); the documents are otherwise the same.

## Who we are
Northwind Inc. makes NorthwindFlow, a project-management app for teams. Founded in 2019, we have about 120 employees and over 1,000 customer teams.

## Your first week
- **Laptop:** Pick up your new laptop on your first day at the IT desk (3rd floor).
- **VPN:** Install NorthwindConnect via HelpHub, sign in, approve in your authenticator, and connect to Northwind-HQ. Update to version 5.1 or newer by October 15, 2026.
- **Tools:** Request tool access (PeoplePort, NorthwindFlow, etc.) via HelpHub → Request access. Manager approval takes about 1 business day.
- **Time off:** 20 vacation days per year (prorated in year one) and 8 sick days. Request time off via PeoplePort at least 2 weeks ahead for vacations longer than 3 days.

## What's happening now
- **Q4 Hiring Plan:** Recruiting for open Q4 roles (2 Support Specialists, 2 Software Engineers, and 1 IT Support Technician).
- **Updates:** NorthwindFlow 3.2 shipped recently, and hybrid work continues with up to 3 days a week from home.
- **Office Reminder:** The kitchen renovation runs October 12–23; use the 2nd-floor break room during this time.

## Who to ask for what
- Product plans and release notes: Product team
- Customer tickets and support playbook: Support team
- Budgets, vendors, and spending: Finance team
- IT helpdesk and VPN: IT team (it-help@northwind.example or extension 4357)

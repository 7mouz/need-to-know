# PixelFlow Product Roadmap (internal)

> Fictional demo data.

**Not public.** Product and Engineering only. Don't share dates with customers.

| Version | Target date | What's in it |
|---|---|---|
| 3.3 | Tuesday, November 17, 2026 | Fix the calendar export time issue (KI-33), better calendar sync |
| 3.4 | **Tuesday, February 23, 2027** | **Offline mode:** view and edit tasks with no internet on desktop and mobile. Changes sync when you're back online |

## Why offline mode now

- It's the most requested feature: 9 support requests this quarter alone.
- It fits the 2027 "work anywhere" theme.

## Open question

- Two people editing the same task offline: the plan is "last edit wins" plus a notice. To be confirmed at the design review on October 20.

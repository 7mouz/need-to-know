# Support Playbook

> Fictional demo data.

## Who handles what

| Level | Who | Handles |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Support specialists | How-to questions, sign-in and account problems, billing questions |
| Tier 2 | Senior support | Bugs, data that looks wrong, anything Tier 1 can't solve in 30 minutes |
| Engineering on-call | Engineers | Outages and lost data |

## Reply times

- Team plan: first reply within 4 business hours.
- Starter plan: first reply within 1 business day.
- Outage: update the status page within 15 minutes.

## How to escalate

1. Check the known-issues list first.
2. Tier 1 → Tier 2: move the ticket to the "Tier 2" queue in Support Desk. Include the app version, device, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot.
3. Tier 2 → Engineering: open an "ENG-ESC" ticket and post the link in the #eng-oncall channel.

## Feature requests

Tag them "feature-request." The Product team reviews tagged tickets every month. Don't promise dates to customers; say "I've passed this to our Product team."

## Tone

Friendly, short, and clear. Thank the customer, give numbered steps, and say what happens next.
